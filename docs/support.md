# Rise — support

Rise is a grace-forward companion for Christian men fighting for sexual
purity. It needs no account and keeps everything on your phone.

> **If you're in crisis or thinking about harming yourself,** call or
> text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or go to your nearest
> emergency room. Rise is a companion, not emergency care.

## Common questions

**Where is my data?** Only on your phone, in the app's own storage. There
is no account and no copy anywhere else.

**How do I move to a new phone?** Make a backup first: open Rise, tap the
person icon on Today, then **Make a backup**, choose a passphrase, and
save the file to Files or iCloud Drive. On the new phone, tap **Restore**
in the same place, pick the file, and enter the passphrase. Restoring adds
your history and never erases anything. A forgotten passphrase can't be
recovered; that's what keeps the file private.

**Reminders don't arrive.** Open Rise, tap the person icon on Today, and
check that the reminder is on. Then check that notifications are allowed
for Rise in your phone's Settings.

**"Tell my allies" doesn't send anything.** It opens your Messages app
with the text ready; nothing is sent until you tap Send. Allies are added
on the Today screen.

**Can my allies see what I log?** No. They only ever receive the short
"I'm being tempted" text, and only when you send it.
