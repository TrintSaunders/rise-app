# Rise

**Though the righteous fall seven times, they rise again.** (Proverbs 24:16)

Rise is a companion for Christian men fighting for sexual purity, built on
mercy, not shame.

- **SOS**, one tap away: breathe, read a verse, follow your own battle
  plan, and let your allies know you're being tempted.
- **Every day**: a verse in the morning and a 30-second check-in at night.
- **After a fall**: Rise Again. Grace first, history never erased.
- **Patterns** that show when it's hardest, and a suggestion worth trying.
- **The Armory**: memorize Scripture, alone or with a friend.

No account, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.
