# Rise — privacy policy

Rise is built so there is almost nothing to say here. It works entirely
on your device.

**What Rise stores:** your name and life verse, your check-ins, logged
struggles and victories, your folders and verse memory progress, your
allies' names and phone numbers, and your settings. All of it lives in
the app's own storage on your device. It is never sent anywhere, and it
is never visible to us.

**Accounts:** there are none. No sign-up, no email, no password.

**Allies and SOS:** when you tell the app you're tempted, it opens your
own Messages app with a short text pre-written; nothing is sent until
you press Send yourself, and it goes over your normal SMS, not through
us.

**Reminders:** scheduled locally on your device. No server involved.

**Backups:** a file the app makes on your device, encrypted with a
passphrase only you know. You choose where to save it. We never receive
it and can't open it.

**Sharing memory folders:** only by a code you copy and hand to someone
yourself.

**Analytics and ads:** none. No tracking, no third-party analytics SDKs,
no advertising identifiers.

**Scripture:** quotations in the app are from the ESV® and NASB® (see
the Armory screen for the full copyright notices).

**Deletion:** delete the app and everything is gone — there is no copy
anywhere else, because there is no anywhere else.

**Contact:** support@riseagainapp.com
