What it does

There in the moment it matters.

SOS, one tap away

Breathe with it, read a verse, and follow the way out you chose yourself, in your own order. One tap tells your allies you’re being tempted. Never why.

Every day

A verse in the morning and a 30-second check-in at night. Honest days are counted first, because telling the truth is the muscle that wins.

Rise Again

After a fall, the sun comes back up. A short guided confession, the clean count starts over, and your history is never erased.

Patterns, told gently

When it’s hardest, what usually comes first, and one practical thing worth trying. Facts and suggestions, never verdicts.

The Armory

Hide the Word in your heart with gentle spaced repetition. Start with verses for the fight, and memorize together with a friend.

Allies

Up to five people who will pray when you text. They don’t need the app, and they only ever hear what you choose to send.

Private by design

Your story lives on your phone. Nowhere else.

No account, no servers, no analytics in the app. The only things that ever leave your phone are messages you send yourself. Back it up to a file only your passphrase can open.
