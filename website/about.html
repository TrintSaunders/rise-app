About

Help in the body.
Hope in God.

Temptation isn’t only a thought. It hits the body: a racing heart, a restless night, a habit your hands know by memory. Rise exists to help a man get through those minutes, and to turn his eyes to the Father who is waiting for him on the other side of them.

Why we built Rise

Most men fighting for purity aren’t short on guilt. They’re short on help in the moment, and on hope after it. Shame tells a man to hide from God. The gospel tells him to run toward Him. We built Rise so that every screen points the same direction: home.

So Rise does two things at once. It helps your body ride out the wave: slow breathing, getting up and moving, putting the phone in another room, calling a brother. And it keeps pointing you to God: His Word in your mind, honest prayer on your lips, and His mercy waiting when you fall.

“No temptation has overtaken you that is not common to man. God is faithful, and he will not let you be tempted beyond your ability, but with the temptation he will also provide the way of escape, that you may be able to endure it.”

1 Corinthians 10:13

Rise isn’t the way of escape. God is. Rise is just there to help you find the door.

What we believe

  • God is the pointRise is a signpost, not a savior. Every feature exists to turn you toward Jesus, not to keep you in an app.
  • Your body mattersYour body is a temple of the Holy Spirit (1 Corinthians 6:19). Temptation is felt in the body, so the help has to be physical too: breath, movement, distance, rest.
  • A fall is a turning pointConfession brings a man back to God instead of deeper into hiding. “If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us” (1 John 1:9).
  • No one fights aloneGod gives us brothers. Rise makes it one tap to let the men you trust know it’s hard (James 5:16).
  • The Word renews the mindFences help, but Scripture hidden in the heart does more (Psalm 119:11). Rise helps you carry it.
  • Sacred privacyYour confessions are between you and God. They live on your phone and nowhere else. No account, no tracking, nothing sold.
