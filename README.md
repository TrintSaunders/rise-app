# Rise *(working title)*

A grace-forward companion app for men fighting for sexual purity — built on mercy, not shame.

> "Though the righteous fall seven times, they rise again." — Proverbs 24:16

## Cornerstones

1. **Grace over shame** — the screen shown after a fall is the most loving screen in the app
2. **Honesty over streaks** — we celebrate truth-telling, not perfect records
3. **Allies over willpower** — the app's real product is the relationship with the allies who stand with you
4. **Formation over filtration** — fill the mind (Phil 4:8), don't just fence it
5. **Sacred privacy** — local-first storage; a man's confessions live on his device unless he chooses to share
